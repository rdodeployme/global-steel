Upgrading? We'll take the old equipment.

Aged-care beds, hoists, wheelchairs, walkers, commercial kitchen stainless and steel furniture — collected from retirement villages and aged-care homes, recycled and reported. We plan the job around your residents. One service fee, quoted before we come.

What we take

Care equipment, kitchens and unit refits

Homes replace beds and hoists on a cycle. Villages refit units as residents move on and upgrade clubhouses, kitchens and gyms. Most of what comes out is steel, stainless or aluminium — and none of it should go to landfill.

  • Aged-care & electric bed frames
  • Bed rails & overbed tables
  • Patient hoists & lifters
  • Wheelchairs
  • Walkers & rollators
  • Shower chairs & commodes
  • Trolleys & linen cages
  • Stainless kitchen benches & sinks
  • Ovens, ranges & dishwashers
  • Fridges & freezers
  • Lockers, shelving & compactus
  • Steel furniture frames
  • Gym & workshop equipment
  • Fencing, gates & balustrades
On site

Before we come

A short list, done by your team

Equipment that residents have used needs to be clean and safe to handle. These are the checks we ask for — the same ones we ask of hospitals.

Beds, hoists, wheelchairs and walkers

  • Cleaned and signed off by your team, with our decontamination declaration signed.
  • Batteries out of hoists, electric beds and mobility equipment. Lithium batteries travel separately, and lead-acid batteries go to a licensed battery recycler.
  • Mattresses, bedding and soft furnishings removed so we collect the frames.
  • Asset register updated on your side before disposal.

Kitchens, laundries and offices

  • Emptied and cleaned — food, linen, files and resident records cleared out first.
  • Fridges, freezers and air-conditioners degassed by an ARC-licensed technician before collection.
  • Computers and servers have their drives removed or wiped, with the data declaration signed.
  • No asbestos. Check your register before a refit — older heaters, linings and fittings can contain it.
Aged-care homes and villages each have their own policies. Send us yours when you book and we will work to them. We do not take clinical waste, sharps, chemicals, oxygen cylinders or asbestos — the full list is here.
