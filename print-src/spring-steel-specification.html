Recycled steel

Processed high-tensile spring steel

Recovered from mattress spring units at our plant in Victoria. Shredded, separated by magnets, vibration, suction and an electrostatic fibre separator, then supplied loose or baled. One input, one product, one process.

The process

  1. Shred. Twin-shaft shredders break the mattress down to steel, foam, fibre and fabric.
  2. Magnetic separation. Three magnets pull the ferrous fraction from the shredded stream.
  3. Vibration and suction. Vibrating decks and high-volume suction strip fabric and foam; dust extraction keeps the air clean.
  4. Electrostatic separation. An electrostatic fibre separator removes the last light fibre.
  5. Check and bale. Inspected, then loaded loose or pressed into bales. Every bale counted before dispatch.

What we do not claim

We do not currently provide independent laboratory testing, chemical composition analysis, moisture testing or certified bulk-density figures. Buyers are welcome to run their own assessment on a supplied sample.
