For retailers

Your take-back, collected and counted

Appliance, hardware, nursery and outdoor retailers handle a steady flow of metal: old appliances taken back on delivery, damaged and returned stock, end-of-line displays and fixtures from refits. We collect it from stores and distribution centres on a regular run, and report the tonnes recycled for each site, a number you can put in your sustainability reporting and in front of customers.

Talk to us about take-backMetals we recycle
  • Every store on one arrangement. The same terms and the same record across the network.
  • Tonnes by site. A recovery statement per collection, rolled up for the whole business.
  • Refrigerant handled properly. Fridges, freezers and air-con are degassed by a licensed technician before processing.
