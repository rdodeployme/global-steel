Collections

Metal collected free. Specialist items quoted.

Old bed frames, filing cabinets, lockers, shelving, racking and steel furniture — if it is a metal-only load, we collect it free. Laptops, servers, medical and lab equipment and anything that needs dismantling carry a service fee, quoted before we come. We do the lifting either way.

Frames, cabinets, lockers, shelving, racking, stainless, cable and scrap — collected free over our minimum load. No skip hire, tip fees or levy.

Specialist itemsQuoted upfront

IT and e-waste, medical and lab equipment, dismantling, small loads and difficult access carry a service fee — quoted before we come.

What carries a service fee

  • IT and e-waste. Laptops, desktops, servers, screens, printers and network gear — priced per item or per pallet, because e-waste has to go through licensed processing.
  • Medical and lab equipment. Cleared items that need careful handling and separating before the metal can be recycled.
  • Dismantling. Unbolting benches and racking, taking apart machinery and fixed equipment.
  • Small loads. Metal-only loads under our minimum.
  • Access and timing. Stairs, long carries, after-hours work and long site inductions.

What counts as a metal-only load

Equipment that is all, or almost all, metal: bed and trolley frames, filing cabinets, lockers, shelving, racking, stainless benches, cable and scrap. A minimum load applies — we confirm it when you book.

Not sure? Items with timber tops, upholstery, plastics or electronics are quoted case by case. Send a photo with your booking and we will tell you what is free before we come. See what we collect.

How it works

From the ward or the lab to the record

Book

Two minutes online. What it is, roughly how much, where it is on site and when suits. Photos help.

Confirm

We confirm what is free and quote anything that is not. Big or complex jobs get a site visit first. Nothing is booked until you approve.

Collect

Our crew does the lifting and loading and follows your site rules and inductions. Drives must be out or wiped before we arrive.

Weigh & recycle

Every load crosses the weighbridge at North Geelong. Metal is processed back into production; e-waste goes to licensed recyclers.

Report

A statement for each collection: what was collected, the weight, what it became and the carbon avoided — ready for your sustainability report.

What you get back

Paperwork you can hand to anyone

  • Weighbridge docket for every load.
  • Collection statement — items, weights, what each became, and the CO₂-e avoided.
  • Data declaration — your signed record that drives were removed or wiped before collection.
  • Period statements across all your sites, in your own Metal Recycling Connect portal.

Quick answers

Before you book

Is the collection really free?

Yes, for metal-only loads over our minimum load: no charge for booking, the truck, the trip or the weight. IT and e-waste, medical and lab equipment, dismantling, small loads and difficult access carry a service fee, quoted before we come.

Where do you collect?

Our crews collect across Geelong and Melbourne. Brisbane and the rest of Australia by arrangement — tell us where.

What about hard drives and patient or student data?

We do not take data-bearing drives. Your team removes or wipes drives and SSDs before collection, and you sign a short data declaration when you book. Our crews will not load loose drives.

Can you take an X-ray machine or other medical equipment?

Yes, as a quoted item, once your team has decommissioned and cleared it — decontaminated, and for X-ray units, made inoperable, oil and lead assessed, and disposal notified to the Department of Health as your licence requires. Your licence holder signs our clearance form. The conditions.

What can't you take?

Asbestos, radioactive material, clinical and biological waste, sharps, chemicals and gas cylinders. Fridges, freezers and air-conditioners must be degassed first. The full list.
