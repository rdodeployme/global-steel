What we collect

If it's mostly metal, we'll take it

Equipment and furniture that is mostly steel, stainless, copper, aluminium or brass — working or not. Items marked Quoted carry a service fee; everything else is collected free in a metal-only load over our minimum. If yours isn't on the list, ask.

Data-bearing equipment

Your data stays with you

Laptops, desktops, servers, copiers and some medical equipment hold data. We do not take data-bearing drives. Before we come, your team removes the hard drives and SSDs, or wipes them under your own process.

  • Remove or wipe. Under your own policy — many organisations follow the Australian Signals Directorate's media-sanitisation guidance.
  • Sign the declaration. A short form when you book, confirming the drives are out or wiped. It is kept with your collection record.
  • Loose drives stay with you. Our crews will not load them.

Need certified destruction for the drives you keep? Ask when you book and we will point you to a provider.

Conditions

A few things need doing before we arrive

Medical & lab

Decontaminated, with a certificate

Equipment that has been in contact with patients, specimens or chemicals is cleaned and signed off by your team first. X-ray units have extra steps.

Batteries

Taken out and kept apart

Lithium batteries are removed and travel separately — they start fires in trucks and shredders. UPS and lead-acid batteries go to a licensed battery recycler, not on our truck.

Gas & oil

Degassed and drained

Refrigerant must be recovered by an ARC-licensed technician before fridges, freezers or air-con are crushed. Oil-filled equipment needs a PCB-free result.

What we can't take

Not on our truck

These need a specialist. If you are unsure whether something qualifies, send a photo and we will tell you before you book.

  • Asbestos — or anything that may contain it, such as old lab benches, fume-cupboard linings and heaters. Check your asbestos register before a strip-out.
  • Radioactive material — sealed sources, nuclear-medicine equipment and anything carrying a radiation trefoil that hasn't been cleared.
  • Clinical and biological waste — including sharps, specimens and contaminated consumables.
  • Chemicals, solvents and oils — and equipment still holding them.
  • Gas cylinders — return them to your gas supplier.
  • MRI magnets — by arrangement only, after specialist decommissioning.
  • General waste — paper, food, packaging and non-metal furniture. That belongs in your normal waste service.
  • Explosives, ammunition and pressurised containers — no exceptions.

Not sure about something?

Send us a photo with your booking. We will confirm what is free and quote anything that is not, before we come.
