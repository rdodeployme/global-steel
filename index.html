Where it goes

Back into Australian production

The metal we collect is processed by us — steel, stainless, copper, aluminium and brass, each kept to its own stream — and sold to mills, foundries and manufacturers. That end market is what keeps the collection free.

Recycled steel

Processed at North Geelong to a defined specification for Australian mills.

Non-ferrous metal

Copper from cable and motors, aluminium, stainless and brass — sorted by grade.

For buyers

Buying recycled metal? Loose, baled or containerised, with a statement on every dispatch.

Part of Recycle Group

Backed by real infrastructure

The Metal Recycling Company is the metals business of Recycle Group — the Victorian resource-recovery group behind The Mattress Recycling Company, Recycle North Geelong and Australia's largest polystyrene plant. The group runs its own weighbridge, processing plant and trade recycling team at North Geelong — so the metal we collect is weighed and processed by us.
