Who we collect from

Wherever the old equipment is

Villages upgrade rooms. Hospitals refit wards. Universities refresh labs and server rooms. Offices and councils strip out floors. Most of what comes out is metal — and none of it should go to landfill.

Retirement villages & aged care

Beds, hoists, wheelchairs and walkers, kitchen stainless and unit-refit steel. We plan the job around your residents.

Hospitals & health

Beds, trolleys, sterilisers, stainless, plant and retired medical equipment — once your biomedical team has cleared it.

Server rooms

Racks, cabinets, servers, network gear and copper cable. Drives out or wiped before we come.

Universities & TAFEs

Lab and workshop clear-outs, IT refreshes, shelving and steel furniture.

Schools

Lockers, shelving, workshop machinery and steel furniture from refits and campus upgrades.

Offices & government

Filing cabinets, compactus, desk frames, depot steel and fencing — through tenants, facility managers and councils.

Where it goes

Back into Australian production

The metal we collect is processed by us — steel, stainless, copper, aluminium and brass, each kept to its own stream — and sold to mills, foundries and manufacturers. That end market is what keeps the service fee down.

Recycled steel

Processed at North Geelong to a defined specification for Australian mills.

Non-ferrous metal

Copper from cable and motors, aluminium, stainless and brass — sorted by grade.

For buyers

Buying recycled metal? Loose, baled or containerised, with a statement on every dispatch.

Part of Recycle Group

Backed by real infrastructure

The Metal Recycling Company is the metals business of Recycle Group — the Victorian resource-recovery group behind The Mattress Recycling Company, Recycle North Geelong and Australia's largest polystyrene plant. The group runs its own weighbridge, processing plant and trade recycling team at North Geelong — so the metal we collect is weighed and processed by us.
