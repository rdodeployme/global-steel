Who we collect from

Wherever the old equipment is

Councils clear depots and parks. Road crews replace guardrail and signs. Households clear garages and old whitegoods. Offices strip out floors. Retailers clear returns and refits. Warehouses change their racking. Hospitals, villages and campuses replace equipment. Factories, workshops and farms retire machinery. Kitchens, solar sites, airports and ports upgrade. Most of what comes out is metal, and none of it should go to landfill.

Councils & parks

Depots, parks and streets: signs, bins, playgrounds, bollards, benches and depot steel.

State roads

Road operators and maintenance contractors: guardrail, sign posts, light poles, culverts and bridge steel.

Homes & garages

Garage, shed and renovation clear-outs: BBQs, mowers, bikes, ladders, trampolines and old whitegoods.

Transfer stations

Drop-off and hard-waste metal on a regular run: whitegoods, bikes, roofing, bed frames, gas bottles and mixed steel.

Health, X-ray & aged care

Hospitals, clinics and retirement villages: X-ray and imaging units, beds, hoists, wheelchairs and stainless, once medical equipment is cleared.

Education

Universities, TAFEs and schools: lab and workshop clear-outs, lockers, shelving and steel furniture.

Offices & government

Strip-outs and relocations: filing cabinets, compactus, desk and partition frames, depot steel and fencing.

IT & server rooms

Racks, cabinets, servers, network gear and copper cable. Drives out or wiped before we come.

Whitegoods & retail

Take-back appliances, returns, damaged stock and shop fittings from stores and distribution centres.

Warehousing & logistics

Pallet racking, roll cages, stillages, shelving and pallet jacks from warehouses and freight depots.

Industry & trades

Machinery, motors, plant, cable, copper pipe and offcuts from factories, workshops and fit-outs.

Automotive & machinery

Panel beaters, workshops and dealers: engines, gearboxes, rims, panels, forklifts and plant, fluids drained.

Farms

Gates, troughs, fencing, tanks, silos and old machinery from farms and rural contractors.

Hospitality

Kitchen replacements and closures: stainless benches, ovens, coolroom panels, shelving and kegs.

Solar

Solar panels collected. Valuable resources recovered. Panels, frames, inverters, cable and battery storage.

Airports & ports

Containers, baggage trolleys, chain, crane and mooring steel. We collect it. We recycle it. We report it.

Report

Recorded and reported

Four numbers for every collection

Weighed in. What it was turned into. How much metal was recovered. The carbon that saved. We record all four for every collection, and you see them in your own portal, with statements your sustainability team can hand to a board, an auditor or a funder. Environmental reporting comes through YARTA Environmental Sustainability, a Recycle Group initiative.

Where it goes

Back into Australian production

The metal we collect is processed by us (steel, stainless, copper, aluminium and brass, each kept to its own stream) and sold to mills, foundries and manufacturers. That end market is what keeps the service fee down.

Recycled steel

Processed to a defined specification for Australian mills.

Non-ferrous metal

Copper from cable and motors, aluminium, stainless and brass, sorted by grade.

For buyers

Buying recycled metal? Loose, baled or containerised, with a statement on every dispatch.

Part of Recycle Group

Backed by real infrastructure

Recycled is the metals business of Recycle Group, the Victorian resource-recovery group behind The Mattress Recycling Company and Australia's largest polystyrene plant. The group runs its own weighbridge, processing plant and collection crews, so the metal we collect is weighed and processed by us.
