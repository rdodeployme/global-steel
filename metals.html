Metals

Ferrous and non-ferrous, from one facility

Two product lines. Spring steel and ferrous scrap off the mattress line and the sorting floor; copper, aluminium, brass and stainless recovered from cable, appliances and commercial clearances. Every stream weighed, processed and reported the same way.

Recycled steelCopperAluminiumStainless & brass

Recycled steel

Processed high-tensile spring steel, ~99% ferrous, loose or baled, 130–250 t a month — plus sorted ferrous scrap. Specification, forms of supply and what we don't claim.

Non-ferrous

Copper and cable, aluminium, brass and stainless — segregated by type, accumulated to lots, sold by weight. Cable granulated mechanically, never burnt.

Stream by stream

What comes in, what it becomes

The same eight streams Global Steel Connect records against every load.

Mattress spring steelShredded; steel pulled by magnets, cleaned by vibration, suction and electrostatic separation → processed spring steel, loose or baled
Ferrous scrapSorted, sized where needed, contamination removed → processed ferrous scrap, baled
CopperSorted by grade, accumulated to lots → sorted copper
CableGranulated mechanically, copper separated from insulation → copper granules
AluminiumSorted by alloy family, baled → sorted aluminium
BrassSorted from plumbing, fittings and hardware → sorted brass
Stainless steelKept separate from the ferrous line, sorted → sorted stainless
Mixed metalsHand and magnet sorted into steel, copper and aluminium → sorted metals by type

Why one facility matters

Same weighbridge, same line, same record

Because every stream is weighed in and out at the one site, Global Steel can say exactly what a load was, what it became and where it went — for the partner who sent it and the buyer who received it. That is not usual in scrap. It is the point of Global Steel.
