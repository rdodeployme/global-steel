National processing & supply

Processed anywhere in Australia

Our plant is in Victoria. For the right volume we can arrange processing anywhere in Australia, closer to where the metal is, then supply buyers in every state by road and rail, or pack containers for export.

Talk to us about processingRequest a sample

Reach

Processed where it makes sense. Supplied Australia-wide.

Because the steel comes off a line that runs every working day, we can commit to a monthly volume and a delivery schedule rather than selling whatever a yard happens to have.

  • ProcessingAt our Victorian plant, or arranged anywhere in Australia where the volume suits, so metal is not trucked further than it needs to be.
  • RoadLoose or baled loads by tipper, flat-top or curtain-sider to any Australian address. Full loads preferred; part loads by arrangement.
  • RailContainerised loads to interstate terminals for buyers set up to receive by rail.
  • ExportBales packed into containers at our site and delivered to the wharf. Documentation prepared for the buyer's freight forwarder.

How to buy

Four steps to your first load

  1. Enquire. Tell us what you buy, where you are and roughly how much you need each month.
  2. Sample. We send a physical sample so you can assess it against your own requirements.
  3. Confirm. We agree form (loose or baled), volume, schedule and freight.
  4. Dispatch. Loads leave our plant on the agreed schedule, by road, rail or container.
